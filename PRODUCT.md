# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People evaluating an asset-swap flow, including product designers and engineers reviewing its visual, interaction, and motion fidelity.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate a focused transition from a single “Start a swap” action into a USDC → ETH form, readiness checklist, connected-wallet form, review, two-step authorization, progress, and receipt. The amount remains editable after the simulated wallet connection and carries through the flow. No real wallet or blockchain action occurs.

## Brand Personality

Crisp, direct, and friendly. The interface uses familiar swap conventions, restrained neutral surfaces, and one confident magenta action color.

## Anti-references

Do not add generic Web3 gradients, neon-on-dark styling, glassmorphism, decorative crypto imagery, bounce, or extra application chrome. Do not invent controls or workflow states beyond the supplied Paper frames.

## Design Principles

- Let the state transition explain where the form came from.
- Treat the Paper geometry, asset pair, rate, and displayed precision as the source of truth. Write the screens as a real swap flow; document the illustrative runtime separately.
- Keep the single magenta action visually rare and decisive.
- Prefer familiar, quiet product patterns over decorative novelty.
- Make the motion optional without making the state change ambiguous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use native form, label, input, output, and button semantics; preserve visible keyboard focus; announce the calculated output without interrupting typing; avoid opening the touch keyboard unexpectedly; and provide an immediate reduced-motion path.
